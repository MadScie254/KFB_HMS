Downtime paper forms

Print blank copies in advance. Urgent care follows hospital policy.

Manual reference and reconciliation

  1. Before a sheet is used, the designated custodian assigns its unique number from a controlled paper register. Write that number on the sheet and in the register.
  2. Record the actual event time, patient identifiers, staff name and signature. Keep completed sheets secure.
  3. After service returns, an authorised staff member checks for an existing patient, payment or stock entry before entering anything through the normal workflow. Write the system reference and entry time on the paper sheet.
  4. A second authorised person checks the paper against the system record, signs the register, and retains the original under hospital policy. Escalate any mismatch before posting a possible duplicate.

These sheets have no automatic numbering, duplicate check or electronic back-entry screen.
