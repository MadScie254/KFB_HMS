# Downtime paper reconciliation

The four printable sheets are blank paper forms. The application does not issue paper numbers, import these sheets, or check them for duplicate posting. `DowntimeEntry` reserves a unique paper-reference field in the schema but has no application writer or reconciliation screen. Follow the hospital's approved clinical and cash handling policy during an outage.

1. The designated custodian assigns a unique sequential reference from a controlled paper register before a sheet is used. Record the sheet type, issuing staff member and issue time in that register.
2. The responsible staff member records the actual event time, relevant patient or transaction identifiers, and signs the sheet. Keep completed sheets and the register under restricted access.
3. Once service is restored, an authorised member of staff checks existing patient, invoice, payment and stock records for the same event before using the normal application workflow. If the event is already recorded, note its system reference on the paper sheet and do not post it again.
4. If the normal workflow cannot faithfully represent the event or its original time, stop and escalate the discrepancy to the owner or reviewer. Do not invent a current-time event to make the paper balance.
5. A second authorised person compares each paper sheet against its system reference, notes the entry and review times, signs the register, and records unresolved differences for investigation. Retain original sheets under the approved records policy.

Payment sheets are not system receipts, and M-PESA references written during downtime remain unverified claims until the normal independent review is complete. Batch stock movements must be checked against the ledger before any later entry to avoid a second deduction.
