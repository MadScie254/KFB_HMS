# Role quick guides

## Reception / cashier

Open a counted shift before cash collection. Search before registering a patient, then start a visit separately. For a pharmacy order, collect only against its exact invoice. Cash clears immediately; manual M-PESA remains unverified until independent reconciliation. Reprint the same receipt—never enter payment again because a printer failed. Close with an actual cash count and explain variance.

## Clinician

Confirm two identifiers and allergy status. Save work as a server draft, then sign when complete; signed notes are not overwritten. Request departmental work from the encounter. Emergency care and clinical discharge may proceed with a documented override even when a balance remains.

## Nurse

Use the configured ward board and patient identifiers. Record observations with units and actual event time. Medication administration is given/not given/withheld with dose and reason; it does not deduct pharmacy stock a second time after custody issue. Complete a named handover.

## Pharmacy

Prepare a priced basket, then direct the customer to reception. Do not collect payment or mark it paid. Dispense only the actual quantity after server clearance. Check product, batch and expiry; the system blocks expired/quarantined stock and prevents a repeated dispense from posting twice. Returns enter quarantine until authorised disposition.

## Laboratory / imaging

Work from requested orders, record performer and result, and release only when authorised. Release is a clinical action, not an automatic result of payment. Amendments must retain the original record.

## Procurement

Create supplier/order evidence and record quotations or reference price. You may enter receipt details, but a different authorised person must approve the order and independently check a delivery/discrepancy. Recording supplier payment is bookkeeping—not a bank transfer.

## Reviewer

Review evidence, not personalities. A discrepancy is not proof of theft. You cannot approve your own transaction. Record the decision and reason for refunds, credits, procurement, stock/cash variance and emergency exceptions.

## Owner

Use reports for oversight and open the underlying authorised records. Unverified M-PESA, unapplied deposits and operating result are kept separate. Review freshness before relying on remote figures. Do not use owner approval for every routine action; appoint a trusted independent reviewer.
